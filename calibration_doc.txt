The company "Solaris Tech" was founded in 2014 by Dr. Elena Vance. 
The headquarters are located in Zurich, Switzerland.
The current CEO is Marcus Thorne, who joined the company in 2018.
The flagship product is the 'Helios Array', a high-efficiency solar collector.
The secret internal project name for the 2026 expansion is 'Project Aurora'.
The company's mission is to provide sustainable energy to remote alpine regions.
